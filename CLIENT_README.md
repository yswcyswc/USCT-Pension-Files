# Zooniverse Guide for Dr. Fields Black

## What This Part of the Project Does

Zooniverse is the human review stage of this project. Each item shows one page image and a draft transcript so that a person can check the text against the original document and correct it.

## How It Fits into the Pipeline

The project moves in a simple sequence:

1. A page is digitized and prepared for review.
2. The system creates a draft transcript.
3. That page and its draft transcript are loaded into Zooniverse.
4. You and your research partners review, correct, and confirm the text.
5. The corrected results can then be exported for later research use.

Zooniverse is where collaborative checking happens.

## How You Can Use It

You can use Zooniverse yourself to:

- spot-check pages
- review difficult handwriting or unclear text
- confirm that the workflow is showing the right page and draft text together
- see how volunteers or partners will experience the material

You can also share it with research partners and encourage them to help validate pages by:

- correcting transcription mistakes
- filling in missing words
- checking names, dates, and places carefully
- flagging pages that are hard to read or may need special attention

## What to Tell Research Partners

You can describe the task simply:

"Each page comes with a draft transcript. Please compare the draft to the image and correct anything that is inaccurate, incomplete, or unclear."

Helpful reminders:

- the page image is the main source
- the AI draft is only a helper
- uncertain readings should be handled carefully
- consistency matters, especially for names, dates, and historical terms

## What Counts as Validation

For this project, validation means that a person has checked the transcript against the page image and improved it where needed.

That might include:

- fixing misread words
- restoring omitted text
- correcting punctuation when it affects meaning
- making sure personal names and place names are accurate
- confirming that the transcript actually matches the page being shown

## Why This Step Matters

Historical documents often include:

- irregular handwriting
- faded ink
- unusual spelling
- damaged pages
- names and terms that automated tools often misread

## What Happens After Zooniverse

Once pages have been reviewed in Zooniverse, the resulting transcriptions can be gathered and used for:

- research analysis
- historical interpretation
- quality checking
- future project stages that depend on more reliable text

## In Short

Zooniverse allows you and your research partners to review page images, improve draft transcripts, and produce more dependable text for later historical research.
